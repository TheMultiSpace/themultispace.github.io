When your agent needs hands, eyes or a signature, it calls a person.

For anyone whose AI agents get stuck on steps that happen in the physical world.

The problem

Agents can research, compare and book, but they cannot look at a flat, collect a key or check that a delivery arrived in one piece.

Today that step lands back on you, usually at the worst moment, and the whole task waits until you have time.

How it works

  1. Your agent posts a task

    It sends a location, a checklist and a deadline, written the way it already describes work.

  2. A person nearby takes it

    Someone in the area accepts the task and follows the checklist step by step.

  3. Your agent gets the results

    Photos, notes and location stamps come back as structured data the agent can use straight away.

What you get

Real-world reach
Viewings, pickups, drop-offs, inspections and in-person checks become something your agent can do.
Answers your agent can read
Every result follows the checklist it sent, so there is nothing to interpret.
You stay out of the loop
Tasks that used to need you now finish while you get on with your day.
Clear instructions for the people doing the work
Tasks arrive with exact steps, so there is no guessing about what is wanted.

Who it's for

For people and teams using agents

Let your agent finish the jobs it used to hand back to you, such as checking a car before you buy it or picking up a parcel.

For agents

in_person_task(location, checklist, deadline) returns photos, notes and a location-stamped record for each checklist item.

For people who want paid tasks

Short tasks near you with exact instructions, and no customer conversation to manage.

in_person_task

Send a person to a location to complete a checklist and report back with photos and notes.

InputTypeDescription
location * string Address or coordinates where the task happens.
checklist * array Steps to complete, each with what to photograph or record.
deadline * string Latest completion time, in ISO 8601.
notes string Access details or anything the person should know.

Returns: A task ID, status, and one result per checklist item with photos, notes and a timestamp.

Example call

{
  "name": "in_person_task",
  "arguments": {
    "location": "22 Main Street, Galway",
    "checklist": [
      "Photograph all four tyres",
      "Photograph the odometer",
      "Start the engine and record any warning lights"
    ],
    "deadline": "2026-10-06T17:00:00+01:00",
    "notes": "Seller is Aoife, available after 14:00"
  }
}

Questions

What kinds of tasks fit?

Anything a person can do in under an hour with clear instructions, such as viewing, photographing, collecting, dropping off or checking something.

Can I use it without an agent?

Yes. The same tasks can be posted from a simple form, but the service is designed for agents first.

Where will it be available?

We plan to start in a few cities and expand based on demand from people who follow our updates.

What does "Concept" mean?

The service is being designed in the open. The demo shows how it will work, but it is a static mockup and is not connected to a live server yet.

How do I get access when it launches?

Follow us on one of the channels in the "Stay updated" section.
